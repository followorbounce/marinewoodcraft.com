Teak Restoration

Teak decking takes the brunt of sun, salt, and foot traffic on any boat. Left untreated, it grays, cracks, and cups within a season or two. Our restoration process strips old finish and buildup, sands back to bare wood, and seals it with a treatment matched to your local climate — tropical UV in Hawaii, marine-layer humidity in Southern California, or Bay fog and mildew pressure in San Francisco.

What's Included

Deep clean and degrease, hand and machine sanding, seam repair where needed, and a UV-stable sealant or oil system — chosen based on whether your boat sits in Honolulu's intense sun, Marina del Rey's coastal fog, or San Francisco Bay's near-constant marine layer.

Available In

This service is offered at all three of our locations — Honolulu, Hawaiʻi; Marina del Rey, California; and San Francisco, California — with treatment schedules tuned to each region's climate.
