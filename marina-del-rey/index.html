Marina del Rey FAQ

Common Questions

Do you restore teak decks for boats docked in Marina del Rey?

Yes — Marina del Rey Harbor is our home base, and we work directly with slip holders across its yacht clubs and marinas. Most inspections can be scheduled within a few days.

Do you travel to Long Beach or Newport Beach for boat wood restoration?

Yes, we regularly service vessels in Long Beach, Newport Beach and San Pedro in addition to our home port of Marina del Rey.

How is Southern California teak maintenance different from other regions?

SoCal's marine layer and airborne salt create a slower but steady degradation cycle compared to tropical climates. Most vessels here need a full restoration every 18–24 months.

Can you handle a full brightwork refinish on a yacht based in San Pedro?

Yes — we take on full strip-and-refinish brightwork projects for vessels based anywhere along the LA and Long Beach harbor system, including San Pedro.
